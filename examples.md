# Customer Support Workflow Examples

This file demonstrates how the AI Customer Support Assistant can be used to analyze different customer situations and support a customer service professional.

## Example 1 — Login Issue

### Customer message

> "I've been trying to reset my password for the past hour, but the link keeps giving me an error. I have an important meeting soon and really need to access my account."

### AI analysis

**Issue:** The customer is unable to reset their password and access their account.

**Sentiment:** Frustrated and urgent.

**Priority:** High.

**Suggested response:**

> "I'm sorry you're having trouble getting back into your account, especially with an important meeting coming up. Let's get this resolved as quickly as possible. Please confirm whether you're seeing an error message after clicking the password reset link, and I'll help you with the next step."

**Next action:** Check the exact error message and escalate to the appropriate technical team if the standard recovery process does not resolve the issue.

---

## Example 2 — Delayed Order

### Customer message

> "My order was supposed to arrive yesterday, but I still haven't received it. Can you tell me what's happening?"

### AI analysis

**Issue:** The customer's order has not arrived by the expected delivery date.

**Sentiment:** Frustrated but neutral.

**Priority:** Medium.

**Suggested response:**

> "I'm sorry your order hasn't arrived as expected. I understand how frustrating it can be when a delivery is delayed. Let me help you check the current status of your order and determine the next best step."

**Next action:** Verify the order status and delivery information before providing an update.

---

## What this demonstrates

These examples show how a structured AI workflow can help a support professional:

- Quickly identify the customer's main issue
- Recognize sentiment and urgency
- Draft an empathetic response
- Determine an appropriate next action
- Keep a human support professional involved in the final decision
